2. How to Sign In
Open your browser to the login page:
Next.js app: http://localhost:3000/login
SvelteKit app: http://localhost:5173/login
Enter the email and password above.
Upon clicking Sign In, the system validates your Argon2id hash, issues an encrypted HTTP-only session cookie (ps_session), and automatically routes you based on your role:
admin / moderator 
→
→ automatically redirected to /admin
owner 
→
→ automatically redirected to /dashboard
visitor 
→
→ redirected to / (or your requested return page)
3. Creating a New Account

You can also register a new Business Owner or Visitor account directly:

Go to /register
Click the "Business Owner" tab
Fill in your name, email, phone number, and password
Click Create Business Account — you will be registered and taken straight to /dashboard.

NOTE

Make sure PostgreSQL is running locally on port 5432 (pimplesaudagar_dev). If you ever need to re-seed or reset accounts, run:
