2. How to Sign In
Open your browser to the login page:
http://localhost:5173/login

Enter the email and password above.
Upon clicking Sign In, the system validates your Argon2id hash, issues an encrypted HTTP-only session cookie (ps_session), and automatically routes you based on your role:
- admin / moderator → redirected to /admin
- owner → redirected to /dashboard
- visitor → redirected to / (or your requested return page)

3. Creating a New Account

You can also register a new Business Owner or Visitor account directly:
1. Go to /register
2. Click the "Business Owner" or "Visitor" tab
3. Fill in your name, email, phone number, and password
4. Click Create Account — you will be registered and taken straight to your dashboard.
